Reading notes
  • Journeys are observable from Jul 19, 2026 (analytics export start) and the lead↔session key from Jul 26 — earlier leads appear in counts but carry no journey.
  • Roughly one in five in-coverage leads shows no journey: corporate machines commonly block tracking. Their absence skews journey stats toward smaller, less locked-down visitors.
  • ICP = matched routing rule for the enterprise team, or the CRM's ICP classifier (company match / enterprise tier) where the rule is unobservable.
  • Booked ≠ held: attendance is not reliably recorded at the scheduler, so the funnel stops at booked/canceled.
