Reading the headlines. Sessions and form-reach come from the analytics warehouse; leads, ICP and bookings from the scheduler and CRM; signups from billing. Form-reach means the session touched the demo-request page — submission and booking are only observable on the lead metrics. Known undercount: a consent-banner change on Aug 12 drops the session id on a growing share of pageviews (~20% by the week of Aug 17), so recent session counts read low — treat session week-over-week as directional and lean on the lead metrics.
