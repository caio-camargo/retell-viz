How to read these numbers

  • Marketing site only. The same analytics property also collects the product app — unfiltered, www is 8.5% of pageviews. Every number here is filtered.
  • Stages are not one chain. Each stage has its own population and window; dividing one stage by another manufactures a fake rate.
  • Analytics can't count conversions — it sees ~10% of real form submissions (ad blockers, scheduler intercepts). Submissions and bookings here come from the form and scheduler archives, which are complete.
  • "% of visitors that are ICP" is not computable. Company identification resolves a biased minority of traffic. ICP numbers are counts, or rates over the identified subset with that stated.
  • Meeting attendance is dark — no-show status is logged on 2 of 2,917 meetings. Cheapest unlock in the whole funnel: reps logging outcomes, one click per meeting.
